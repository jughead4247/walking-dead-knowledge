About The Walking Dead Quiz

The Walking Dead Quiz is a 50-question trivia challenge for fans of The Walking Dead television series. The quiz tests how well you remember the survivors, communities, groups, locations, relationships and major events that shaped the story.

The questions cover both major storylines and smaller details that can be easy to forget after watching a long-running series. You may be asked about characters, occupations, communities, alliances, conflicts, important locations and memorable events from different periods of the show.

Each question has four possible choices. A correct answer earns 2 points, giving the quiz a maximum score of 100. Your final percentage is used to determine your Walking Dead Knowledge level and show how well you remember the television series.

The quiz is focused on the original television series. It is not intended to test knowledge of the comic books or the entire collection of The Walking Dead spin-off shows.

How The Walking Dead Quiz Works

The quiz contains 50 questions, with four possible answers presented for each question. Read the question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct before moving to the next one.

Questions can cover many different parts of the series. Some may test your memory of major characters and their relationships, while others focus on communities, locations, groups, occupations or specific events. Familiarity with the overall story can help, but some questions are designed to test detailed knowledge.

Every correct answer is worth 2 points. With 50 questions, the highest possible result is 100 points. Your final result is displayed as a score out of 100 along with a Walking Dead Knowledge level.

There is no official passing grade. A high score simply means that you remembered a large amount of information from the series. If you watched the show several times, remember minor characters and can recall events from different storylines, you may have an advantage.

The quiz is intended for entertainment and trivia. It is not an official test produced by AMC or the creators of The Walking Dead.

Quiz Navigation

Starting the quiz

Select START QUIZ on the opening screen to begin. The first question will then appear with four possible choices.

Selecting an answer

Read all four choices before making your selection. Think about the characters, locations, relationships or events mentioned in the question before deciding which answer is correct.

Moving between questions

Use the Next button to continue through the quiz. The Back button allows you to return to an earlier question and reconsider your answer before submitting the quiz.

Completing the quiz

Answer all 50 questions and select the submit option when you reach the end. Your final score will then be displayed along with your Walking Dead Knowledge level.

Try Again

Select TRY AGAIN after receiving your result if you want to replay the quiz. You can use another attempt to improve your score or see whether you remember questions that challenged you previously.

Challenge Your Friends

After completing the quiz, use CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to share the challenge and compare your Walking Dead knowledge with other fans.

The Walking Dead Quiz FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

The quiz contains 50 questions, and every question has four possible choices.

How is the score calculated?

Each correct answer is worth 2 points. With 50 questions, the maximum possible score is 100.

What does the Walking Dead Knowledge level mean?

The knowledge level is based on your final quiz result. It provides a simple way to describe how well you performed based on the number of questions you answered correctly.

Does the quiz cover the original TV series?

Yes. The quiz is designed around the original The Walking Dead television series and includes questions from different periods of the show's story.

Does the quiz include the comics?

No. The questions are based on the television series. The TV adaptation differs from the comic books in characters, relationships and story events, so comic knowledge may not always match the answers in this quiz.

Does the quiz include the spin-offs?

The main focus is the original television series. The quiz is not designed to cover every character, event or storyline from the wider The Walking Dead spin-off universe.

What are walkers?

Walkers are the reanimated dead that threaten the survivors throughout The Walking Dead. The characters use several different terms for them, although "walker" is the most recognizable term used throughout the series.

What is considered a good score?

There is no official passing score. A result above 80 indicates that you correctly answered most of the questions and have strong knowledge of the series. Lower scores simply mean there are more details left to remember.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after receiving your result to restart the quiz and make another attempt.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Use the CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS button after completing the quiz to share the challenge and compare your results with other fans.

Spoiler Warning

This quiz contains spoilers for The Walking Dead, including characters, relationships, communities, major events, conflicts and other story details from different periods of the television series.

If you have not finished watching the series and want to avoid learning important story details, consider watching the relevant episodes before taking the quiz.
