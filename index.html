About The Walking Dead Quiz

The Walking Dead is one of television's best-known post-apocalyptic dramas, following survivors trying to rebuild their lives after a mysterious outbreak turns the dead into aggressive reanimated walkers. The television series premiered in 2010 and ran for eleven seasons, concluding in 2022.

Based on the comic book series created by Robert Kirkman, Tony Moore and Charlie Adlard, the television adaptation developed its own characters, storylines and major plot changes. This means that remembering the comics alone is not enough to answer many questions about the television series.

This 50-question quiz focuses specifically on details from The Walking Dead television universe. Questions cover characters, communities, locations, groups, occupations, identities, relationships and memorable events. Some questions test famous moments, while others focus on smaller details that dedicated viewers may remember.

The quiz includes characters such as Rick Grimes, Daryl Dixon, Carol Peletier, Michonne, Glenn Rhee, Maggie Greene, Negan, Ezekiel and many others. It also covers groups and communities including Alexandria, Hilltop, the Kingdom, the Saviors, the Whisperers and the Commonwealth.

Every question has four possible answers. A correct answer is worth 2 points and an incorrect answer is worth 0 points, giving the quiz a maximum score of 100. Your final result places your performance into a Walking Dead knowledge level, from a casual viewer to a Walking Dead Encyclopedia.

The Walking Dead Trivia and Frequently Asked Questions

What are walkers in The Walking Dead?

Walkers are the reanimated dead that threaten the survivors. The show uses several different names for them, including walkers, biters and geeks, depending on the character or group using the term. The word "zombie" is notably uncommon within the show's own dialogue.

What are some of the main communities in the series?

Communities become increasingly important as the series develops. Alexandria provides a protected residential community, Hilltop becomes an important agricultural settlement, and the Kingdom is led by Ezekiel. Other major locations include Oceanside, the Sanctuary and the Commonwealth, each with its own leadership, rules and conflicts.

Who are the major groups in The Walking Dead?

The survivors encounter many organized groups throughout the series. These include the Saviors led by Negan, the Whisperers led by Alpha, the Scavengers associated with Jadis, the Wolves and the Civic Republic Military. The different groups demonstrate how societies can develop very different rules and power structures after a collapse.

Is The Walking Dead only about zombies?

No. The walkers are a constant threat, but much of the story focuses on human relationships, leadership, trust, survival, morality and the rebuilding of society. Conflicts between survivor groups often become just as important as the danger posed by the dead.

Does this quiz include the entire television series?

The questions draw from a broad range of the television series, including early Atlanta-era events, the prison, Woodbury, Terminus, Alexandria, the Saviors, the Whisperers and later communities such as the Commonwealth. The quiz therefore includes both famous storylines and smaller details from different seasons.

Does the quiz include Walking Dead spin-offs?

The main focus is the original television series. Some questions may involve information connected to the wider television universe, but this quiz is primarily designed for viewers familiar with the original The Walking Dead series.

A higher score means you remembered more of the show's characters, locations, groups and events. Scores above 80 indicate strong knowledge, while very high scores suggest that you remember many of the obscure details as well as the major storylines.
